A family travel game about making your flight

Airport Panic is a fast, funny time management game about family travel chaos. Tom, Priya, Maya and Ollie Bramley are off on holiday, and someone has to get all four of them, and their luggage, from the bedroom floor to seat 14A. That someone is you. Every level is a 2–5 minute run against one boarding countdown.

Five airport mini-games, one boarding clock

  • Packing: a suitcase packing game. Drag and rotate clothes, chargers and a toy dinosaur into the case. Starred items must go in.
  • Bag Drop: match bags to owners, fix an overweight bag and spot the wrong boarding pass.
  • Security: trays roll through the scanner. Tap the items that should not be there before the belt runs out.
  • Gate Dash: slide to steer the family through the terminal, dodge trolleys and grab time bonuses.
  • Boarding: passports, boarding groups and one last head count before the door closes.

Twenty random events, from gate changes to a snack emergency, keep every run different. Earn up to three stars per level.

Free to start, no ads, plays offline

Levels 1–10 are free. One optional one-time purchase unlocks the full game: every remaining level and every level added in future updates. No adverts, no energy timers, no in-game currency and no subscription. No account or internet connection needed to play.
