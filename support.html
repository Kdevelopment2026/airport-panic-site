Frequently asked

Is it free?

Levels 1–10 are free. One optional one-time purchase unlocks the full game forever: every remaining level and every level added in future updates. There are no subscriptions, adverts or in-game currencies.

I bought the Full Game but it is locked again

Open Settings → Full Game → Restore Purchases while online. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and restore on any of your devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every level plays offline and progress is saved on your device. Only the purchase and restore steps need a connection.

Accessibility

Settings includes Reduce Motion, independent music and sound-effect volumes, a haptics toggle and high-contrast scanner targets. Every game state is shown with an icon or shape as well as colour, and no task needs sound to complete.

Are the airport rules real?

No. Packing, baggage, security and boarding rules in the game are fictional and simplified for fun. They are not travel or security guidance. All airlines, airports and people in the game are fictional.

Delete my data

Airport Panic stores progress and settings only on your device. Deleting the app deletes everything. We hold no account or server data about you.
