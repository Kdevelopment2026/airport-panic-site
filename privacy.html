Privacy Policy

Airport Panic · Effective 6 September 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

The short version

Airport Panic does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no server. Everything the game remembers (level progress, stars, settings, whether you own the Full Game) lives only on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Level progress, stars and best scores.
  • Settings: music and sound volume, haptics, Reduce Motion, high-contrast targets.
  • Whether the Full Game purchase has been unlocked (a cached yes/no so the game works offline).

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app. It is not backed up by us; it may be included in your own iCloud or iTunes device backup, which is controlled by Apple and your settings.

In-app purchases

The optional Full Game unlock, and any optional tip, is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details and no personal information about you from Apple. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. Restore Purchases asks the App Store which products your Apple ID owns; the app only receives the result.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No advertising and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

The game is suitable for a family audience and collects no data from anyone, including children. Optional purchases are protected by Apple's standard purchase confirmation and any Screen Time or Ask to Buy settings on the device.

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that collects data (for example optional leaderboards), this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first, and any such feature will be off by default.
